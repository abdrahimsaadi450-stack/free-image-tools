What Is JPG to PNG Conversion?

JPG to PNG conversion changes an image from JPG or JPEG format into PNG format. PNG is useful when you need a lossless image format for graphics, designs, and digital projects.

This free converter works directly in your web browser. No software installation or account is required.

How to Convert JPG to PNG

  1. Select a JPG or JPEG image.
  2. Click the "Convert to PNG" button.
  3. Wait for the conversion to finish.
  4. Click "Download PNG Image".
