PNG vs JPG

PNG is a lossless image format that supports transparency. JPG uses compression and is commonly used for photographs and images where a smaller file size is useful.

When converting PNG to JPG, transparent areas cannot remain transparent because JPG does not support transparency. This converter uses a white background for those areas.
