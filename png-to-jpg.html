What Is PNG to JPG Conversion?

PNG to JPG conversion changes an image from PNG format into JPG format. JPG is widely used for photographs, websites, social media, and digital images.

This free PNG to JPG converter works directly in your web browser. You do not need to install software or create an account.

How to Convert PNG to JPG

  1. Select a PNG image from your device.
  2. Click the "Convert to JPG" button.
  3. Wait for the conversion to finish.
  4. Click "Download JPG Image".

Why Convert PNG to JPG?

  • JPG is widely supported by websites and applications.
  • JPG files are often smaller than PNG files.
  • JPG is commonly used for photographs.
  • JPG works well for many social media platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. The PNG to JPG converter is free to use.

No. The conversion works directly in your web browser.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image conversion is performed directly in your browser. The tool does not require uploading your image to an external image-conversion service.

Will the JPG file have the same dimensions?

Yes. The converted JPG keeps the original image width and height.

What happens to transparency?

JPG does not support transparent backgrounds. Transparent areas are converted to a solid background during the conversion.
