Free AI Background Remover

Remove unwanted backgrounds from photos and images using AI. The processing is performed directly in your browser, so the selected image does not need to be uploaded to an image-processing server.

The result is provided as a transparent PNG that you can download and use for designs, thumbnails, product images, social media graphics and other projects.

How to Remove an Image Background

  1. Click "Choose an Image".
  2. Select a JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP image.
  3. Click "Remove Background".
  4. Wait while the AI processes the image.
  5. Preview the transparent result.
  6. Click "Download Transparent PNG".

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the background remover free?

Yes. The tool is provided free of charge.

Are my images uploaded?

The image processing runs in the browser. Your selected image is not intentionally uploaded to our own server for processing.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images are supported.

What format is the result?

The result is a PNG image with a transparent background.

Why can processing take some time?

AI background removal runs on your device. The first use can take longer because the required AI files must be downloaded and prepared in the browser. Later uses can be faster because browser caching may be used.
