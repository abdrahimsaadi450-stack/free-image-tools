Free AI Background Remover

Remove backgrounds from photos automatically using an AI image segmentation model running directly in your browser.

No account is required and the image is processed locally in the browser after the AI model has loaded.

The tool can work with people, animals, products and many other foreground subjects.

How to Remove a Background

  1. Select an image.
  2. Click "Remove Background".
  3. Wait while the AI model processes the image.
  4. Check the transparent preview.
  5. Download the PNG image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover free?

Yes. The tool is free to use.

Are my images uploaded?

The image is processed directly in your browser after the AI model has loaded.

Does it use AI?

Yes. This version uses an AI image segmentation model to generate the background mask.

Why is the first use slow?

The AI model must be downloaded by your browser the first time. The model is much larger than normal JavaScript code, so the first load can take some time.

What format is the result?

The result is downloaded as a PNG image with a transparent background.
