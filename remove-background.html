Free Online Background Remover

This free background remover can make a simple image background transparent directly in your web browser.

The tool compares image pixels with the background color found in the top-left corner of the image. Pixels that are sufficiently similar to that color are made transparent.

For the best results, use an image with a simple and relatively uniform background.

How to Remove a Background

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Make sure the top-left corner contains the background color.
  3. Adjust the background removal strength.
  4. Click "Remove Background".
  5. Check the preview.
  6. Download the transparent PNG.

Tips for Better Results

  • Use images with a plain background.
  • Use a background with a consistent color.
  • Make sure the top-left corner represents the background.
  • Increase the strength if too much background remains.
  • Decrease the strength if parts of the subject become transparent.
