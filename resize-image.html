What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the width and height of an image. It can be useful when you need an image with specific dimensions for a website, social media, documents, thumbnails, or other digital projects.

This free image resizer works directly in your web browser. No software installation or account is required.

How to Resize an Image

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter the desired width in pixels.
  3. Enter the desired height in pixels.
  4. Click the "Resize Image" button.
  5. Download your resized image.

Benefits of Resizing Images
