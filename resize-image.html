What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the width and height of an image. It is useful when you need an image with specific dimensions for a website, social media post, profile picture, thumbnail, document, online store or other digital project.

This free image resizer works directly in your web browser. No software installation or account is required.

How to Resize an Image

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter the desired width in pixels.
  3. Enter the desired height in pixels.
  4. Click the Resize Image button.
  5. Download your resized image.

Why Resize Images?

Different websites and digital platforms often require images with specific dimensions. Resizing an image can help you prepare files for websites, social media, thumbnails, documents and other online uses.

Changing the pixel dimensions can also make an image easier to fit into a specific design or layout.
